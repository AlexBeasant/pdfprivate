Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. PDF Private runs entirely in your web browser. Your file is read into your device's memory, processed by code running on your device, and saved straight back to your downloads. It never travels over the internet, so we never see it. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit and no paid tier. Because your device does the work, there are no server costs to pass on.

How can PDF tools work without uploading?

Modern browsers can run the same kind of PDF software that used to need a server. PDF Private loads open-source PDF libraries (PDF.js and pdf-lib) from this website, then does all the work on your own device.

Do you track me?

No. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and no cookies. Every file the site needs, including fonts, is served from pdfprivate.com itself, so no other company is contacted when you visit.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once you have visited a tool, it is saved by your browser and works without an internet connection. You can also install PDF Private as an app from your browser's menu.
